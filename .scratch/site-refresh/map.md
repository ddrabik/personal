# Reimagine David Drabik's Photography Portfolio

## Destination

Produce high-fidelity, browser-reviewable mocks for the home and About pages that express David's identity as a landscape and wildlife photographer and are convincing enough to support a decision about production implementation and live publication.

## Notes

- Read [`CONTEXT.md`](../../CONTEXT.md) before working a ticket; use its audience and photography vocabulary.
- Use `/grilling` with `/domain-modeling` for content and product decisions, and `/prototype` for questions that require something visual or interactive.
- The primary audience is the lookup visitor; recruiters are secondary.
- Begin with the existing portfolio selection. Birds and sunsets are signature subjects.
- The home page should offer immersive exploration rather than reproduce a conventional or Instagram-like gallery.
- A blazing-fast initial view and deliberate access to higher-resolution images are standing constraints.
- The desktop experience is fullest. The touch experience may reduce spatial complexity but must remain coherent on iPad and phone.
- Wayfinder is planning this effort through decision artifacts and throwaway prototypes. Production implementation is not authorized by this map.
