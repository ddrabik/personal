# Photography Portfolio

The portfolio presents David Drabik's public identity and selected photographic work.

## Language

**Photographer**:
David's public creative identity, centered on landscape and wildlife photography.

**Landscape photograph**:
A photograph in which natural scenery or the environment is the primary subject.

**Wildlife photograph**:
A photograph in which an animal in its environment is the primary subject.

**Bird photograph**:
A wildlife photograph whose primary subject is one or more birds.

**Sunset photograph**:
A landscape photograph centered on the light, color, or atmosphere around sunset.

**Signature subject**:
A recurring subject that expresses one of David's strongest photographic interests; currently birds and sunsets.

**Lookup visitor**:
The portfolio's primary audience: someone searching for David after encountering his name or meeting him socially.

**Recruiter**:
A secondary audience for whom the portfolio demonstrates that David has substantial interests beyond his professional work.

**Portfolio selection**:
The curated set of photographs already showcased on the current site and retained as the initial body of work for the refresh.

**Photographer profile**:
The personal context behind David's work: his general story, length of photographic experience, gear, and ways to contact him through Instagram or email.

**Immersive exploration**:
A nontraditional, spatial way of discovering the portfolio selection in which interaction is part of the experience.
_Avoid_: Gallery, when referring to the intended browsing experience

**Desktop experience**:
The fullest form of immersive exploration, designed for a desktop browser and precise pointer input.

**Touch experience**:
The coherent iPad and phone adaptation of immersive exploration, designed for touch input and permitted to reduce spatial complexity.
